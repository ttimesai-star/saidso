    What a receipt proves — and what it does not

    Proves

    • The question, answer, model, time and note are exactly what was sealed. Any edit — even one character — is detected and named.
    • Every receipt with the same signer fingerprint was sealed by the same browser key.

    Does not prove

    • That an AI really wrote the answer. SaidSo seals what you paste; only the AI provider could sign its own output.
    • The time. It is signed, but it comes from the sealer's clock.
    • Who the signer is. Compare the fingerprint with one the person shared with you.
